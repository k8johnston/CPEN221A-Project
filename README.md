# CPEN221A-Project: Study Buddy

## Mission Statement
Advancing student connectivity and academic collaboration across UBC.

## Problem Statement
Students at UBC have no means to connect and study together, and as a result, they often study alone. However, group studying is typically more effective at facilitating learning. While some online platforms do exist with the purpose of connecting students, they are either poorly made or aren't tailored to life at UBC. This ultimately prevents users from properly connecting with their peers. This issue particularly affects newer students, who may struggle to meet others that they can study with. Without academic connection students' grades may suffer. However, their community engagement can also affect mental resiliency and overall quality of life.
